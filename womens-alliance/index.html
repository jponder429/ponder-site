Why AIF

Practical judgment. Not policy theory.

Most AI ethics training is written for regulators and researchers. AIF is written for the advisor who used a chatbot to draft a client email this morning. Common sense first. Scenario-based. Built around the decisions you actually face, not abstractions you'll never use.

Who It's For

If you're using AI to draft client communications, research markets, or summarize filings, you're already making ethics calls. AIF gives you a framework for making them well. No technical background required.
